AUTOMATION CHALLENGE

Automation Challenge is an automation competition that tests participants' ability to design, program, and develop microcontroller, PLC, or IoT-based systems to solve a given case study. Participants are challenged to produce functional, creative solutions and to implement an automation system through the process of design, programming, testing, and troubleshooting. In the final stage, participants will implement and demonstrate their system live according to the level and category of the competition.
