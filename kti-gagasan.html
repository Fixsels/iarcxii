VORTEX COMPETITION

The Idea track asks participants to freely identify an issue relevant to the theme, then propose an original, well-reasoned idea or conceptual solution direction. No case study is provided by the committee at this stage — the strength of this track lies in originality and depth of reasoning, not proof of implementation. The Innovation track asks participants to choose one of two case studies/problem statements provided by the committee (aligned with the research focus of the relevant research center/center of excellence), then develop a systematic, evidence-backed solution including a feasibility analysis and, where possible, a prototype or proof of implementation.
