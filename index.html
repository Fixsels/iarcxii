ABOUT IARC

IARC (Industrial Automation & Robotics Competition) is a competition that brings together students and university students to test their skills in robotics and automation through creative, innovative, and applicable challenges.

IARC serves as a platform for the younger generation to develop technical skills, creativity, problem solving, and teamwork through a technology competition.

FAQ

What is the IARC competition sequence ›
The IARC competition sequence starts from registration, submission/selection, technical meeting, through to the final and winner announcement according to the timeline schedule.
Who can register ›
Participants from elementary, junior high, senior high/vocational, up to university level can register according to the available competition categories.
Is equipment or robots provided by the committee ›
Equipment requirements differ per competition branch — some are provided by the committee, others must be brought by participants according to the guidebook.
Is accommodation available for participants outside Surabaya ›
The committee provides recommended accommodation information around the event location for participants from outside Surabaya.
